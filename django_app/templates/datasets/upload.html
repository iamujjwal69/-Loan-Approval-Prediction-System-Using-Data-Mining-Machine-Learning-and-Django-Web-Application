Expected Schema Specifications

To ensure reliable feature extraction and ML pipeline compatibility, uploaded files must contain the standard Kaggle loan attributes:

  • Loan_ID - Unique applicant record identifier
  • Gender - Male / Female
  • Married - Yes / No
  • Dependents - 0, 1, 2, 3+
  • Education - Graduate / Not Graduate
  • Self_Employed - Yes / No
  • ApplicantIncome - Monthly applicant income
  • CoapplicantIncome - Monthly coapplicant income
  • LoanAmount - Requested credit in thousands
  • Loan_Amount_Term - Term in months (e.g. 360)
  • Credit_History - 1.0 (Meets) or 0.0 (Poor)
  • Property_Area - Urban / Semiurban / Rural
